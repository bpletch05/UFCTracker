Standings are derived from your game picks (most conference wins = 1st). Each team scores 5 minus the distance between predicted and actual finish (floor 0). Penalty: −5 if a team you had top-5 in its conference actually finished in the bottom 5.
CFP — +5 per team correctly placed in the 12-team field, +3 bonus for exact seed, +8 for the correct national champion, +4 for the correct runner-up.
Heisman — 6 minus the distance between predicted and actual rank for each correct top-5 pick (floor 0), +10 bonus for the correct winner at #1.
Ties in derived standings are broken alphabetically for scoring purposes.
